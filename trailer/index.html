About Loon Life

Loon Life is a cozy island adventure experience where you drift in by hot-air balloon, wander through unique islands, and slowly turn an wild island into a place that actually feels like yours. Play at your own pace, decorate how you like, and build a little world that feels like home.

Features

A few of the things you’ll be able to do when you touch down in LOON LIFE:

🎈

Drift & Discover

Travel between islands by hot-air balloon and explore a world that never quite repeats.

  • Discover billions of unique island, each with a unique rune code
  • Each island has its own layout, mood, and resources
  • Drop in for short or long sessions that still feel like an adventure
🍃

Seasons, Weather & Events

Feel the world gently change around you with shifting seasons, cozy weather and special events.

  • Dynamic weather like rain, snow, fog and sunny breaks
  • Seasonal changes affects the islands' look and feel
  • Special events can happen like fireflies, auroras and meteor showers
🏝️

Claim Your Own Island

Find a beautiful island, plant your flag, and slowly turn it into home.

  • Claim an island and shape it from a wilderness to cozy home
  • Place your crafted or newly found builds on your new island
  • Take control of your entire island using its unique portal
🤝

Play & Build Together

Share the world with friends: visit each other’s islands and build side-by-side.

  • Visit friends or open your island for others to drop in
  • Build together on islands and show off your creations
  • Relax, explore, and decorate as a small cozy group
🛠️

Craft, Gather & Grow

Gather resources at your own pace and turn them into items and builds.

  • Mine, chop, harvest to craft new items and builds
  • Grow a farm with fruits, vegetables, and flowers
  • Level your professions to mine, chop and harvest new resources
📜

Quests, Badges & Long-Term Goals

Always have something to chase, without the pressure of daily streaks or timers.

  • Follow optional questlines that guide you through new islands
  • Earn badges for exploration, building, and cozy little milestones
  • Collect memories tied to the islands you’ve touched
