Our Story

Our journey began with a simple idea from the founder. He wanted to learn how to develop games on Roblox and decided to dive in by creating his first title, LOOT RUNNERS. It was the very first project ever made by the studio and served as a playground for learning scripting, design, and the Roblox workflow. Even without an official launch or any marketing, the game still attracted a few thousand players, which was an incredible moment for a completely new creator experimenting with game development for the first time.

During the creation of LOOT RUNNERS, ideas for a second project were already forming. The result became DREAM PLOTS, inspired by the classic era of Minecraft creative plots. The goal was simple: give players the freedom to build anything they could imagine with a huge collection of free models and advanced placement tools. Once again, this project was never officially launched with a marketing push, but it still reached thousands of unique players.

Around this time, the founder revisited one of his older favorite games, Outer Wilds, which is built around exploring a small solar system filled with secrets that slowly reveal themselves. That simple but powerful concept sparked the idea to create a horror game with a similar sense of discovery.

This idea grew into FARSIGHT, a story-driven horror experience shaped by everything learned from the previous projects. Development went smoothly, and the studio experimented with new mechanics, puzzles, and atmosphere to create something special. This was also the first project that received a real trailer and a proper release plan. With only a very small budget behind it, the launch still went well. The game received a steady group of players during the first days, and because players asked for it, a cooperative mode was added shortly after. Over time, the most dedicated fans managed to discover all the secrets and uncover the true ending.

After a full year of building games and learning from the ground up, the studio entered a quieter period. The founder focused on his studies, took on client assignments, and kept improving his development skills. Now that this chapter is complete, it is finally time to return with something new.

LOON LIFE is our next major game. It brings together the spirit of all our past projects and the experience earned over multiple years of Roblox development. The game is planned for release in December 2025 and has already been in development for ten months. It is designed as a living world that will continue to grow with weekly updates and larger monthly content drops.

We are excited to share more soon. The official trailer for LOON LIFE is coming in December this year, and we cannot wait to release our biggest project so far. This will be our first fully marketed title, and we are proud to present something that we believe has never been seen before on Roblox.
