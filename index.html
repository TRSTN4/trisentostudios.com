Our Story

It all started when the founder of the studio wanted to make a game — something that didn’t exist yet. After a lot of brainstorming and development, our first game, LOOT RUNNERS, was released. Not long after, our second project, DREAM PLOTS, came to life — a creative game where everyone could freely build on their own plot using advanced placement tools and tons of free assets.

After Dream Plots, we felt something was missing. We wanted a game with a story — something more adventurous and thrilling. That’s how FARSIGHT was born: a unique horror experience with an intriguing and mysterious storyline.

Now, almost two years after FARSIGHT’s release, it’s time for the next big step. A game that combines everything we’ve learned — one game that connects the spirit of all our previous titles. LOON LIFE is that project. It’s not released yet, but it’s planned for October 25, 2025.

We see our older titles as learning experiences that helped shape who we are today. With LOON LIFE, we’re building something long-term — a living game with weekly updates, new content, and ongoing support for years to come.
